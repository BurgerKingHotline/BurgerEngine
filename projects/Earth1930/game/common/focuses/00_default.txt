# House rules for the trees of this game (Burger's lint lists breaches): no date checks (what lets a country take a focus, or the AI choose it, is the
# situation of the country and the world - never the clock), and no war or conquest effects: a focus may make a demand (make_demand), whose acceptance
# is the other country's decision under the diplomatic action's own rules. The AI picks by ai_will_do; the weights of a historical tree are
# scaled by the Historical Bias in the rule FocusRules.AiWeight (at bias 0 only generic reasons are left).
